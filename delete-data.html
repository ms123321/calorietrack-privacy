Request data deletion (without deleting your account)

CalorieTrack (Google Play: com.gssei.calorietrack) · Developer listing contact applies · Last updated: August 16, 2026

You can request deletion of some or all of your CalorieTrack data without closing your account, or clear data yourself on the device. To permanently close your account instead, see account deletion.

Steps to request data deletion

  1. Email the support / developer email shown on the CalorieTrack Google Play store listing.
  2. Subject: CalorieTrack data deletion request
  3. Include:
    • The email used to sign in (Google or email/password), if you have an account
    • What you want deleted (examples below)
    • Confirm you want to keep the account (unless you also want full account deletion)

What you can clear yourself (no email needed)

  • Delete individual diary / food entries in the App
  • Android: Settings → Apps → CalorieTrack → Storage → Clear data (removes on-device diary and local preferences)
  • Uninstall the App (removes remaining on-device App storage)

Types of data — deleted vs kept

We can delete or help you remove (on request / via device controls):

  • On-device food diary, goals, and preferences (via Clear data / uninstall / in-app edits)
  • Selected cloud-synced profile or diary fields linked to your account (on verified request)

Typically kept or outside our full control:

  • Your login account itself (unless you also request full account deletion)
  • Google Play purchase / subscription records (managed by Google)
  • Short-lived security or technical logs needed for fraud prevention or legal compliance
  • Data already processed by third parties under their policies (e.g. Open Food Facts search queries, ad networks for Free ads) according to those services’ retention rules

Retention period

We aim to process verified data-deletion requests within 30 days. Temporary technical logs related to a request may be retained up to 90 days, then deleted or anonymized.

Request account & data deletion

CalorieTrack (com.gssei.calorietrack) · Last updated: August 16, 2026

You can request deletion of your CalorieTrack account and associated cloud data (for example Firebase Authentication account data and synced profile/subscription information linked to that account).

Delete in the app (recommended)

  1. Open CalorieTrack on your phone.
  2. Sign in with the account you want to delete.
  3. Go to the More tab → Account.
  4. Tap Delete account and confirm.

This removes your Firebase Authentication account, cloud profile data linked to that account, and diary data stored on that device.

Or request deletion by email

  1. Open your email app.
  2. Send an email to the support / developer email shown on the CalorieTrack Google Play listing (or the contact email listed for this app on Play).
  3. Use subject line: CalorieTrack account deletion request
  4. In the body, include:
    • The email address used for CalorieTrack (Google or email/password sign-in)
    • Optional: your Firebase / Google account email if different
    • A clear request to delete your account and associated cloud data

What we delete

  • Your authentication account for CalorieTrack (when we can verify ownership)
  • Cloud-synced user/profile/subscription records linked to that account

What may remain on your device

Food diary and other data stored only on your phone can be removed by clearing app storage in Android settings or uninstalling CalorieTrack. Guest-mode data is typically only on-device.

Timing

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days (or sooner when possible), subject to security verification and legal retention requirements.
